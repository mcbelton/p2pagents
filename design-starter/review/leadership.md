## Summary

- Members get one private list of what they must deal with
- Ready for everyone in 6 weeks, with 2 engineers
- **Ask:** approve the plan and an early customer preview

---

## Why it matters

- Members lose track of what they must do, and miss some of it
- A simple list is the most requested feature
- Goal: 60% of active members use it in their first week

---

## Timeline

- Weeks 1–4: build and test
- Week 5: early customers try it and tell us what to change
- Week 6: available to everyone, step by step

---

## Risks

- **Privacy:** lists may hold personal details; only their owner sees them, and nothing is logged
- **Quality:** a gradual release that we can switch off within minutes

---

## Ask

- Approve the plan and two engineers for six weeks
- Approve the early customer preview in week 5
