# {{title}}: what we're planning, and your feedback

Thank you for telling us how you keep track of what you need to do. Here is what we plan to build, so you can tell us whether it fits before we finish it.

## What we heard

- What you need to do ends up in scattered notes, so some of it gets missed.
- Adding something should take a few seconds.
- Your list should stay private.

## What we're building

- A personal list: type a name, and the item appears at the top.
- Your items, newest first, wherever you sign in.

## How it helps you

- One place for what you need to deal with, so nothing slips.
- Only you can see your list.

## What's not included yet

- Sharing a list with other people.
- Due dates and reminders. Tell us if you need them.

## Questions for you

1. Would due dates or reminders change how you use the list?
2. Is "newest first" the order you want, or would you sort it differently?
3. Do you need to share a list with someone? With whom?
4. What would make you stop using it?

## Next steps

- We will compare your answers with our plan and share an early preview in about five weeks.
- Send your answers to your account team, or ask them for a 30-minute call.
