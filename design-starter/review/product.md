## Problem

Members keep what they must deal with in scattered notes, lose track and miss some of it.

---

## Users and stories

- **US-1** Add an item, so I keep track of it
- **US-2** See my items, newest first, so I know what is left

---

## Prototype walkthrough

1. Add "Renew the parking permit": it appears at the top
2. Press Add with no name: "Name is required"
3. Signed out: a clear "Sign in first" screen

<!-- Open the prototype from the review pack and click through it live. -->

---

## Scope

- **In:** add items, list them newest first, private to each member
- **Out:** sharing lists, due dates (an open question)

---

## Success metrics

- 60% of active members add an item in their first week
- Fewer than 1% of adds fail
- 90% task success in usability tests

---

## Release plan

- The team, then 10% of members, then everyone over a week
- A customer feedback round before everyone gets it
